### Proposed economic objective

For approved capital C and a fixed evaluation horizon, define per-session portfolio net return:

`r_net[t] = sum_i(w[i,t-1] * executable_total_return[i,t]) - variable_cost[t]/C - operating_cost[t]/C`.

Use actual invested-capital accounting in implementation rather than this fixed-C planning simplification when cash flows occur. Include commissions, spread, slippage, financing/borrow if permitted, data, compute, and storage; dividends/corporate actions follow one frozen convention. Report pre-personal-tax results and identify tax assumptions separately. Freeze one cost convention: simulated benchmark/midpoint returns subtract the modeled spread/slippage once; returns reconstructed from actual fill prices already include execution-price effects and must not subtract them again. Explicit fees/financing and fixed operating costs remain separately booked.

Proposed decision objective: maximize expected net log wealth growth `E[log(W_H/W_0)]` subject to approved capital, drawdown, exposure, liquidity, turnover, and loss controls and a wealth model with admissible positive wealth. This is an economic objective, not permission to deploy a Kelly optimizer or apply log utility to an unconstrained Student-t simple-return law with ruin probability. Use a fixed policy for model comparison, not an optimizer tuned on the evaluation period.

Primary economic comparator: costed, equal-weight eligible-universe portfolio under the same cash/exposure convention. Also report cash and an appropriate broad-market total-return benchmark; choose the actual investable benchmark before the experiment, not after results. Separate risk differences from alpha claims.

Proposed practical margin: **2 percentage points annualized net excess return**, evaluated over **252 untouched or prospective sessions**. For H observed eligible sessions define `A_p = product_t(1+r_net_p[t])^(252/H)-1`, `A_b` identically for the costed benchmark, and the primary economic contrast `A_p-A_b`. Also report unannualized horizon wealth and the time series of paired returns. Annualization is a convention, not an independent sample-size multiplier. Resample paired portfolio/benchmark session blocks together and recompute this full nonlinear statistic. Proposed qualification: lower 95% dependence-aware confidence bound exceeds 0.02, all approved risk limits pass, and observed excess return remains positive under a predeclared doubled-variable-cost stress. This may be inconclusive at one year; do not promise that horizon provides sufficient power. A power/precision assessment based only on development data precedes freezing the run.

Unapproved simulation-only defaults for discussion: long-only, no borrowing/shorting/options, gross exposure <=100%, per-name <=5%, per-sector <=25%, daily one-way turnover <=10% of NAV, participation <=1% of trailing traded dollar volume, and stop new risk at 10% peak-to-trough drawdown. These are not live limits or validated optimal choices. Actual capital, liquidity source, turnover convention, portfolio benchmark, daily loss limit, gap-risk handling, and tolerances need approval before decisions are executed. Unknowns do not block data/protocol planning. A drawdown stop cannot prevent a larger realized gap loss.

### Correct risk and causal semantics

For Student-t conditional scale s and degrees of freedom nu, variance is `s^2 * nu/(nu-2)` only for nu>2; `exp(E[h])` is neither complete predictive SD nor generally `E[exp(h)]`. Use posterior predictive draws including the next latent-state transition; do not use smoothed future-conditioned states as forecasts. Prefer explicitly defined quantiles if the model does not support the claimed moment. Separate-head `P(up)<0.5` with positive mean can be coherent under asymmetric returns.

Prediction `P(Y|X,I)` is not identified intervention `P(Y|do(X),I)`. Keep a causal register with a precise estimand, information timing, graph, confounders and identification argument. Predictive edges remain associations absent that argument. An economic feature/causal successor needs a measured deficit and separately frozen hypothesis; sensitivity and placebo checks cannot prove absence of unobserved confounding.

For ETF-prior v1, use holdings known at cutoff, actual weights, visible omitted exposure/cash/tracking residual and joint posterior draws. A fixed-weight approximation has variance `w' Sigma w + Var(residual) + 2 Cov(w'R,residual)`; independence is an assumption, not a default. Do not renormalize observed partial holdings to full exposure. Leveraged/inverse/derivative ETFs need appropriate contracts or research exclusions, not silent application of a cash-equity sum. Reusing stock observations in both a posterior-derived prior and the ETF likelihood requires a justified joint model or disjoint conditioning; naive double counting is prohibited. Match the holdings ablation on the same observations.
